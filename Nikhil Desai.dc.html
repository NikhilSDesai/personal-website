La Rambla Climate Intervention ABM

A pedestrian agent-based model of La Rambla, Barcelona, running current conditions against a proposed climate intervention side by side. Agents seek shade during heat events, so adding tree canopy and cooling amenities changes where people walk, stop and crowd, and the dashboard scores the resulting drop in heat vulnerability.
